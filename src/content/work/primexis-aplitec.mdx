## The clients

**Primexis** is a French financial-management firm providing consolidation and reporting services to large corporate groups and international subsidiaries. **Groupe Aplitec** is a Paris-based accounting, audit and advisory group. Both run on internal tooling that had accumulated over decades.

## The scope

A team of five engineers, led by me at Tanbits, built internal platforms for both firms:

- **Automated their legacy systems.** Accounts and bookkeeping automation replacing manual, spreadsheet-and-legacy-app workflows.
- **AML/CFT compliance checks.** Anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing screening automated in a regulated domain, where a missed check is a regulatory event.
- **Automated data updates and event propagation** throughout the ecosystem. An event-driven architecture keeping each firm’s internal tools in sync instead of relying on people to re-enter data.
- **Government API integrations** with French regulatory and administrative APIs.

## The delivery model

This account is the proof that the full-lifecycle agentic pipeline works end to end:

**PRDs → AI development → AI Playwright testing → AI CI/CD → UAT → final delivery.**

Compound engineering throughout: the requirements, decisions and roadmap lived in versioned files, so the work accumulated across weeks instead of resetting with each session. Builder agents implemented features in parallel, reviewer agents validated them, tester agents produced the unit and end-to-end suites, and CI enforced coverage before anything reached UAT.

A small team, a regulated financial domain, European enterprise clients, and every phase of the SDLC run agentically, not just code generation.
